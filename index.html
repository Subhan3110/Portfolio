About

I'm studying for an MSc in Finance at Imperial College Business School in London (2026 to 2027), focusing on advanced corporate finance, company valuation and private equity. I hold a BSc in Finance from BI Norwegian Business School with a GPA of 4.5/5, with A grades in Corporate Finance, Options and Futures, Financial Analysis and Valuation, and Quantitative Finance.

Alongside my bachelor's I worked as an AML Analyst at KPMG Norway, a Debt Advisor at Kredinor, where I secured repayment agreements in 55% of cases (about 30% above the team average), and a Client Advisor at Nordea, where I ranked among the top performers in a cohort of nearly 200.

I compete in finance case competitions, led analysts as Senior Analyst at BISO Invest, and manage a personal portfolio of Nordic equities and ETFs. On the side I build tools with code and AI, like the study apps below. Outside of finance: racket sports, martial arts, running and strength training.
